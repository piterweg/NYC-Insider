New York in June: long light and free concerts

June is the start of summer: warm days, long evenings and the first free outdoor concerts. It is busy, but also the month when the city's calendar opens up — Pride, Tribeca and outdoor stages.

The short version

The longest days of the year

June gives New York its latest sunsets and its biggest outdoor calendar. The heat has not yet become the story, which makes it one of the better summer months for walking.

Average highs sit around 81°F (27°C) with lows near 64°F (18°C); humidity starts to build but the evenings stay comfortable. Daylight peaks at about fifteen hours around the solstice, so the city stays light until after 8pm.

The month's calendar is the draw: Pride at the end of June, the Tribeca Festival in early June and free concerts across the parks. Book the big sights early and plan outdoor time for the mornings.

What's on

What is on in June

Pride, the film festival and the start of the free summer concerts.

  • 3–12 June 2027 — Tribeca Festival

    Film, talks and events across Lower Manhattan. The next edition is in June 2027; check the festival's site for the current programme.

  • Last Sunday of June — Pride

    The march and street celebrations draw huge crowds; plan transport accordingly and expect road closures.

  • June–August — Free summer concerts

    SummerStage, BRIC Celebrate Brooklyn! and Lincoln Center Summer for the City run free outdoor shows; check each programme for dates.

How to spend it

How to spend a June day

Start early, rest in the heat of the afternoon and go back out for the long evening.

Morning

The park before the heat

Outdoor sights are much more pleasant before noon; Central Park at 8am is a different city.

Afternoon

A museum or the waterfront

The High Line and Brooklyn Bridge Park are best in the morning; the afternoon is for air conditioning.

Evening

A free concert

Check the free summer programme and bring a blanket; most shows start in the early evening.

Practical

June planning notes

June is very busy, with summer holidays beginning and Pride drawing a huge weekend crowd. Book hotels and the big sights early, and expect the parks and waterfronts to be full at weekends.

Light, breathable clothing, sunscreen and a refillable water bottle, plus a light layer for air-conditioned museums. Comfortable shoes for long walks and an umbrella for the odd summer shower.

◷

Use the mornings

Heat and humidity build through the day. Outdoor sights are far more pleasant before noon.

◆

Plan around Pride

The last Sunday of June brings road closures and packed trains in Lower Manhattan; check routes before travelling.

☂

Some free summer shows need a reservation even though they cost nothing; check the programme in advance.

Questions

Month questions

Is June a good time to visit New York?

Yes, if you like long evenings and outdoor events. It is warm rather than brutally hot, though humidity starts to build towards the end of the month.

How hot is New York in June?

Average highs are around 81°F (27°C) and lows around 64°F (18°C), with humidity starting to rise. Evenings are comfortable.

What is on in June?

Pride on the last Sunday of the month, the Tribeca Festival (3–12 June 2027) and the start of the free summer concert season.

Does it rain in June?

About eleven wet days on average, often as short afternoon storms. An umbrella is worth carrying.
