How we write

Reported, not aggregated

Every Journal piece starts from the same question: what would we tell a friend who is arriving on Friday?

That means prices with the date attached, opening hours that reflect the current schedule, and an honest note when something is not worth the money. We do not invent restaurants, we do not pretend a queue is a discovery, and we update the pages when the facts change.

If a recommendation is paid for, it will say so. If we have not been somewhere recently, we describe the category rather than the place. The result is a guide that is occasionally less exciting than the internet and considerably more useful on the day.
