Who we are

We are not a booking site. That is the point.

NYCTrove is written by people who live in and around New York, for people who want to understand it — not just tick it off.

There is no secret layer to New York that only insiders can access. There is only knowing which entrance to use, which hour to visit, which ticket is a genuinely bad deal and which neighbourhood rewards a second stop. That is what we write about.

When we recommend a pass, a tour or a restaurant, we say why — and we say what it is not good for. If we ever earn a commission from a link, it never decides the ranking.

Passes, honestly

Do not buy a sightseeing pass before you read this.

The big three passes are not interchangeable. CityPASS suits a short first trip. Go City's Explorer Pass wins when you want three or four big-ticket sights. The all-inclusive passes only pay off if you genuinely want to be out sightseeing every day.

Our comparison breaks down real 2026 prices, what is actually included, and the break-even point for each pass — plus a calculator that tells you which one wins for your trip.

Insider tip

Buy timed-entry tickets directly from the attraction's own website whenever you can. Third-party resellers charge a booking fee and cannot move your slot if it rains.

★

Free and always worth it

The Staten Island Ferry, the High Line, Brooklyn Bridge Park, Grand Central's concourse, and the free museum hours listed in our budget guide. No ticket, no queue, no catch.
