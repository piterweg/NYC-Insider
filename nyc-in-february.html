New York in February: the quiet cold

February is one of the coldest months in New York and one of the quietest. It is also the best-value window of the winter: low hotel rates, easy museum entry and the last two weeks of Restaurant Week.

The short version

Deep winter, without the crowds

February is winter at its most serious: short days, cold winds and the occasional snowstorm. It is also the cheapest and quietest month of the year, and the museums are blissful.

Average highs sit around 43°F (6°C) with lows near 28°F (−2°C), and February often brings the coldest snaps of the year. Daylight is up to about ten and a half hours by month's end, so there is noticeably more usable afternoon than in January.

The holiday visitors are long gone, Restaurant Week runs until mid-February, and the last weeks of the month are some of the best-value weeks in the city. If you want the Met or MoMA without a queue, this is the moment.

What's on

What is on in February

A quiet month with Restaurant Week and the last weeks of the winter season.

  • Until mid-February — NYC Restaurant Week

    Prix-fixe menus at $30, $45 and $60. Reserve early; the good dinner tables go first.

  • Until 28 February — Bryant Park Winter Village

    The skating rink and market close for the season at the end of the month. Rink admission is free; skate rental is paid.

  • All month — The quietest museums

    February is one of the two quietest months for museums and galleries; the first hour after opening is almost empty.

How to spend it

How to spend a February day

Lean into the cold: museums in the morning, a short walk at midday, a long dinner in the evening.

Morning

A museum without a queue

February is the best month for the big museums. Go at opening and have the rooms nearly to yourself.

Midday

A brisk park walk

Central Park in the low light is beautiful; keep to the south end and the paths around Bethesda.

Evening

An early dinner

Restaurant Week menus and a warm bistro. Book ahead for the final week of the month.

Practical

February planning notes

February is quiet almost everywhere, which makes it the best month for anyone who hates queues. Hotel rates are at their lowest of the year, and the only busy period is the Restaurant Week rush for dinner reservations.

Bring the warmest coat you own, plus a hat, gloves, a scarf and thermal layers. Waterproof boots with grip are essential for slush, and a light sweater is useful because museums and restaurants are heavily heated.

◆

Book the cold-weather sights

Museums, Broadway and long lunches are best in February precisely because everyone else is staying home.

◷

Watch for the coldest snap

February can produce single-digit wind-chill days. Check the forecast and keep an indoor plan in reserve.

☂

Take the long view on daylight

Days lengthen noticeably by the end of the month, so late-February afternoons are good for walking.

Questions

Month questions

Is February a good month to visit New York?

Yes for value and quiet, if you can handle the cold. It is one of the cheapest months for hotels and among the quietest for museums, with Restaurant Week running until mid-February.

How cold is New York in February?

Average highs are around 43°F (6°C) and lows around 28°F (−2°C). Cold snaps can be much colder; pack for wind and possible snow.

What is on in February?

Restaurant Week runs until mid-February, Bryant Park's Winter Village stays open to 28 February, and the museums are quiet all month.

Does it snow in February?

It can, and February often brings the winter's coldest snaps, but heavy snow is not guaranteed. Waterproof boots are more useful than a snow forecast.
