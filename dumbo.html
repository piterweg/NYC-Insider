Brooklyn · DUMBO

DUMBO: two bridges, one perfect view

DUMBO is four blocks of cobblestones between the Brooklyn and Manhattan bridges, and it contains the most photographed view in Brooklyn. It is small, scenic and best done on foot in two or three hours.

First impressions

The view that made the neighbourhood

DUMBO stands for Down Under the Manhattan Bridge Overpass, and the name is the geography: the streets sit in the gap between two bridges, with the river on one side and old warehouses on the other.

The famous photograph is at the end of Washington Street, where the Manhattan Bridge frames the Empire State Building down the block. Go early, before the queue forms at mid-morning, then walk to Brooklyn Bridge Park for the view back at Lower Manhattan.

The neighbourhood itself is tiny: a few cobbled streets, converted warehouses, a carousel and the park. That is enough for half a day, especially if you continue along the waterfront to Brooklyn Heights or walk the bridge into Manhattan.

What to see

What to see in four blocks

DUMBO is small; these four fill it properly.

01

Washington Street

The bridge-framed view down the block toward the Empire State Building. Arrive before 9am if you want a clear shot.

02

Brooklyn Bridge Park

Waterfront lawns, Pebble Beach and the best skyline panorama in Brooklyn.

03

Jane's Carousel

A restored carousel in a glass pavilion beside the river, in the park's northern section.

04

The blocks under the Manhattan Bridge

Cobbled streets and converted warehouses where the old industrial neighbourhood is easiest to read.

An afternoon here

Two hours in DUMBO

Do the photograph first, then the park, then lunch; the neighbourhood is small enough to walk twice.

  • 08:30 — Washington Street

    The famous bridge view is best before 9am, when the light is clean and there is no queue.

  • 09:30 — Brooklyn Bridge Park

    Walk down to the waterfront and along the lawns, looking back at Lower Manhattan.

  • 11:00 — Jane's Carousel and the waterfront

    Ride the carousel or simply sit by the river, then walk the cobbled streets under the bridge.

  • 12:30 — Lunch

    Pizza at Juliana's or the market; expect a wait at weekends.

  • 14:00 — Onward

    Walk the bridge into Manhattan or follow the waterfront south to Brooklyn Heights.

Practical

Getting there and getting it right

The A/C stop at High Street and the F at York Street are both a few minutes from the waterfront. The NYC Ferry ($5 one-way) stops at DUMBO, which is the nicest way in from Manhattan. The neighbourhood is compact and cobbled, so bring comfortable shoes rather than a car.

Early morning is the best time: the Washington Street view is empty, the park is quiet and the light is good. Weekends from late morning are the busiest. A weekday evening is also lovely, when the day-trippers have gone and the waterfront is calm.

◷

Arrive before 9am

The Washington Street view attracts a queue by mid-morning. Before 9am you can take your time and still have the street to yourself.

◆

Do not plan a whole day

DUMBO is two to three hours. Combine it with Brooklyn Heights or the bridge walk rather than stretching it.

☂

Cobblestones and rain

The streets are uneven and get slippery in the wet. Wear shoes with grip, and plan the park or the market for the worst of the weather.

Keep exploring

Nearby and worth your time

DUMBO is the start of the Brooklyn waterfront, so keep walking south or across the bridge.

Questions

DUMBO questions

How long do you need in DUMBO?

Two to three hours covers the streets, Brooklyn Bridge Park and the Washington Street view. Add the bridge walk or a walk along the waterfront and it becomes half a day.

What is the famous DUMBO photo?

The view down Washington Street, where the Manhattan Bridge frames the Empire State Building in the gap between the bridge towers. It is best early, before the queue forms.

How do I get to DUMBO?

The A/C stops at High Street and the F at York Street, both a few minutes from the waterfront. The NYC Ferry also stops at DUMBO, which is the nicest approach.

Is DUMBO good for families?

Yes. Brooklyn Bridge Park has lawns, playgrounds and Jane's Carousel, and the streets are short enough for children to walk. The bridge walk is the bigger outing.
