New York in September: the best month of the year

September is the safest answer to when to visit New York: warm days, cool evenings, the lowest rain count of the year and the summer crowds heading home. It is also when the US Open finishes in Queens.

The short version

Summer without the heat

September keeps the warmth but loses the humidity and the crowds. Days are still in the mid-70s, evenings are cool enough for a jacket, and the light softens into the best walking weather of the year.

Average highs reach 77°F (25°C) with lows around 63°F (17°C), and the month averages fewer wet days than any other. Daylight is about twelve and a half hours, and the parks and waterfronts are at their most comfortable.

The first week still belongs to the US Open in Queens, and the start of the school year marks the point when the city exhales. From mid-September the museums, restaurants and neighbourhoods are easier, which makes it the best all-round month for a first trip.

What's on

What is on in September

The US Open finishes, and the city enters its best-weather window.

  • Early September — The US Open

    The tennis finishes at Flushing Meadows in the first half of the month (2026: 30 August–13 September); check the official site for current dates.

  • September to early October — The best-weather window

    This is the second of the year's two best stretches; book the outdoor sights and restaurant terraces.

  • From mid-September — The city calms down

    Once the school year starts, the summer crowds thin and museums and restaurants become easier to book.

Practical

September planning notes

September starts busy with the US Open and the last of the summer visitors, then calms noticeably from mid-month. The later weeks are one of the best combinations of good weather and manageable crowds in the year.

Light layers, comfortable walking shoes and a light jacket for the evening. September can still deliver a hot afternoon, so keep a summer layer too.

◷

Aim for the second half

If you can choose your dates, mid to late September gives the best weather with far fewer people.

◆

Book the outdoor sights

This is the best month for the High Line, the bridge walk and the waterfront; timed tickets for decks are still worth booking.

☂

Do not write off the US Open

The tournament ends in the first half of September; if tennis is the reason for the trip, plan the first week.

Questions

Month questions

Is September the best month to visit New York?

Many would say yes: warm days, cool evenings, the fewest rain days of any month and smaller crowds after mid-month.

How warm is New York in September?

Average highs are around 77°F (25°C) and lows around 63°F (17°C), with lower humidity than July and August.

When is the US Open?

It normally finishes in the first half of September; the 2026 edition runs 30 August–13 September. Check the official site for current dates.

Is September busy in New York?

The first half is busy with the US Open and late-summer visitors; from mid-month the city becomes noticeably calmer.
