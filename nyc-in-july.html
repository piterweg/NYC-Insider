New York in July: heat, storms and long evenings

July is hot and humid, with highs around 84°F and afternoon storms that pass quickly. The city responds by moving outside in the evening: free concerts, waterfront parks and a restaurant scene that spills onto the pavement.

The short version

Hot days, loud nights

July is the hottest month in New York, and the humidity is what people remember. The trick is to plan the day around the heat: mornings outdoors, afternoons indoors or in the shade, evenings out.

Average highs reach 84°F (29°C) and lows around 70°F (21°C), with about fourteen and three-quarter hours of daylight. Afternoon thunderstorms are common and usually brief, cooling the streets for the evening.

Restaurant Week starts in mid-July, running into mid-August, and the free summer concerts are in full swing. The parks and waterfronts are busy at weekends, but weekdays are easier and the long evenings are the best part of the month.

What's on

What is on in July

Restaurant Week, free concerts and the height of the summer season.

  • Mid-July to mid-August — NYC Restaurant Week

    Prix-fixe menus at $30, $45 and $60 across the city; book as soon as reservations open.

  • All month — Free summer concerts

    SummerStage, BRIC Celebrate Brooklyn! and Lincoln Center Summer for the City continue through the summer; check each programme.

  • All month — The waterfront at night

    The long evenings make the rivers the best place to be after 7pm, with free skyline views.

How to spend it

How to spend a July day

Beat the heat by starting early and staying out late, with an indoor middle to the day.

Morning

Walk before the heat

Start by 8am for the park or the High Line; by midday the pavements are hot and the shade is crowded.

Afternoon

Indoors or on the water

Pick a museum, or take the free Staten Island Ferry for the harbour breeze. Afternoon storms are a good excuse for a long lunch.

Evening

A free concert

The free summer programme is the heart of a July evening; bring water and a blanket.

Practical

July planning notes

July is very busy, with summer holidays in full swing. The big sights sell out, the parks are crowded at weekends and the waterfront is busiest at sunset.

Light, loose clothing, sunscreen, a hat and a refillable water bottle. A light rain jacket for afternoon storms, and comfortable shoes for walking on hot pavements.

◷

Do the outdoor sights early

The city is at its hottest from 2pm to 5pm; do the walking before noon and save the museums for the afternoon.

◆

Carry water and use the shade

There are fountains and refill stations in the big parks. Museums and libraries, with free WiFi and air conditioning, are good midday refuges.

☂

Watch the storm forecast

July storms are short but intense. If thunder starts, move indoors rather than waiting it out under a tree.

Questions

Month questions

Is July a good time to visit New York?

If you can handle heat and humidity, yes: long evenings, free concerts and a lively city. If heat bothers you, May or September are better choices.

How hot is New York in July?

Average highs are around 84°F (29°C) and lows around 70°F (21°C), with humidity that makes it feel hotter. Afternoon storms are common.

What is on in July?

Restaurant Week from mid-July to mid-August and free summer concerts across the parks. The 2026 International Naval Review ran 3–7 July; check the official site for future dates.

Does it rain in July?

About ten or eleven wet days, mostly as short afternoon storms that pass within an hour or two.
