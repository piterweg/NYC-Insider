New York in March: the city starts to thaw

March is a transition month: cold mornings, occasional warm afternoons and a city that is visibly waking up. Temperatures climb into the low 50s, the days lengthen and the tourist crowds are still modest.

The short version

Cold mornings, longer afternoons

March is the least predictable month of the year: one day is a coat-and-scarf day, the next is warm enough for a park bench. The city is still quiet, and the light is finally coming back.

Average highs reach 50°F (10°C) and lows sit around 36°F (2°C), but the averages hide the swings. Daylight stretches to almost twelve hours by the end of the month, and the parks start to show early green.

There are no major festivals in March, which makes it a good month for the sights you would otherwise queue for: the Met, the High Line on a mild day and the quieter neighbourhoods. It is also a calmer month before the spring and summer crowds arrive.

What's on

What is on in March

A quiet month defined by the change of season rather than a calendar of events.

  • All month — The parks wake up

    Early blossom and longer days make this the first month for a proper park walk. Central Park is quiet on weekday mornings.

  • All month — Museum season

    With no major festivals, March is one of the easiest months to see the big museums without a timed-entry scramble.

  • Late March — The light returns

    Daylight reaches almost twelve hours, and evenings become usable again for the first time since autumn.

How to spend it

How to spend a March day

Keep the plan flexible: one indoor and one outdoor option, decided by the morning forecast.

Morning

A museum at opening

Cold mornings are made for the Met or MoMA; both are calm in March.

Midday

The park on a mild day

If the sun is out, walk the Mall to Bethesda Terrace; if not, switch to an indoor plan.

Evening

A neighbourhood dinner

March is quiet enough to walk into most restaurants without a booking, except at the weekend.

Practical

March planning notes

Crowds are moderate: more than January and February, fewer than April and May. Weekends are busier, especially if the weather is good, and the first warm Saturday brings everyone outside.

Pack layers rather than a single heavy coat: a waterproof jacket, a sweater and an umbrella cover both the cold mornings and the mild afternoons. Comfortable waterproof shoes are worthwhile because March rain is common.

Questions

Month questions

Is March a good time to visit New York?

Yes if you want moderate crowds and lower prices than spring and summer. The weather is changeable, so pack layers and keep one indoor plan for each day.

How warm is New York in March?

Average highs are around 50°F (10°C) and lows around 36°F (2°C), with big swings from day to day. Mild afternoons become more common later in the month.

Does it rain in March?

Yes, about eleven wet days on average. A compact umbrella and a waterproof jacket are worth carrying.

What is on in March?

There are no major festivals. The month is best for museums, longer days and the first park afternoons of the year.
