## Passes

- **CityPASS** — $149 adult / $129 child (6–17), + $2 fee. 5 attractions from 10
  (Empire State Building 86th, AMNH + one exhibition, Top of the Rock, 9/11 Museum,
  Statue of Liberty & Ellis ferry, Circle Line, Intrepid, Guggenheim, Edge, Whitney).
  Valid 9 days from first use; reservations required at most sites.
- **Go City Explorer Pass** — from $89 for 2 choices, tiers up to 10 (~$89–$300).
  2–10 of 101 attractions; buy anytime, activate within 1 year, then 30 days to use.
- **Go City All-Inclusive** — from $169 (1 day) to ~$500 (10 days); unlimited entry to
  101 attractions on consecutive days.
- **Go City Essentials** — from $99: 1 observation deck + 2 attractions from 15.
- **New York Pass** — from $89, 1–10 day tiers (~$100–$400), 100+ attractions, consecutive days.
- All operators use dynamic pricing; weekend/holiday dates run 20–40% higher.

## Transit & airports

- Subway/local bus base fare **$3.00** (from 4 Jan 2026). Express bus $7.25. Reduced $1.50.
- Single-ride OMNY ticket $3.50 (2 h validity, no subway↔bus transfer).
- **OMNY fare capping:** 12 fares in any rolling 7 days then free — about **$36/week** on
  subway + local bus; $67 incl. express; $17.50 reduced. Must tap the same card/device.
- **MetroCard sales and refills ended 1 Jan 2026.** Existing cards accepted "into 2026"
  until MTA announces a cut-off. OMNY card $1 introductory fee at vending machines.
- **AirTrain JFK $8.75.** Jamaica → E/J/Z + LIRR; Howard Beach → A. Runs 24/7.
- JFK → Manhattan: AirTrain + E subway **$11.75**, 50–60 min; AirTrain + LIRR $14 off-peak /
  $16 peak, 30–40 min; yellow cab **$70 flat** + tolls/tip (~$90–110), 45–75 min.
- LGA: no rail. Free Q70 bus + subway **$3.00**, 45–60 min; metered cab ~$35–55 + extras.
- EWR: AirTrain + NJ Transit ~$20–25 to Penn, 30–45 min; Coach USA bus ~$18.
- Congestion pricing adds $0.75 per yellow-cab trip and $1.50 per rideshare trip into
  Manhattan south of 60th St.
- PATH $3.25 single (from 4 May 2026); 10-trip bundles $3.10/ride.
- NYC Ferry **$5.00** one-way (from 19 Oct 2026); 10-trip $30; discount $1.50.
- **Staten Island Ferry free**, 24/7, every ~30 min, 25 min each way.
- Citi Bike: $4.99/30 min single, $25 day pass, $239 annual.

## Money & practical

- NYC sales tax **8.875%**; hotel tax ~**14.75% + $3.50/night** on top of the quoted rate.
- Tipping: 18–22% sit-down (20% standard), 15–20% taxis, $2–5 per bag, $1–2 per drink, $3–5/night housekeeping.
- Daily budgets per person excluding hotel: budget **$130–200**, mid-range **$350–550**,
  upscale **$800–1,300+**. Hotels: dorm $60–120, budget $180–280, mid $300–500, upscale $600–1,200+.
- Only ~**150 of 472** subway stations are step-free; all buses are wheelchair-accessible.
  Step-free examples: Times Sq–42 St, Grand Central, Fulton St, Atlantic Av–Barclays,
  Flushing–Main St, 161 St–Yankee Stadium, Coney Island–Stillwell Av.
- LinkNYC free WiFi; free WiFi on subway platforms and in libraries.
- Luggage storage via Stasher/Bounce/Radical Storage ~$6–12 per bag per day; hotel bell desks often free.
- Safety: statistically safe; watch for pickpockets in Times Square and on crowded trains,
  and for fake Statue of Liberty ticket sellers. 911 emergency, 311 non-emergency.
- Apps: MTA, Citymapper, Transit, NYC Ferry, TKTS/TodayTix, Resy/OpenTable, Curb, Uber/Lyft.

## Neighbourhood one-liners (for cards / linking)

- Manhattan: Midtown (icons + theatre, crowded, best first-time base), Lower Manhattan
  (history, 9/11 Memorial, ferries), SoHo (cast iron + shopping), Greenwich Village
  (brownstones, Washington Square, jazz), Upper East Side (Museum Mile), Upper West Side
  (AMNH, Lincoln Center, Zabar's), Harlem (Apollo, Schomburg, soul food, jazz).
- Brooklyn: Williamsburg (waterfront skyline, Bedford Ave, Domino Park), DUMBO
  (Washington St bridge view, Brooklyn Bridge Park), Park Slope (brownstones, Prospect Park),
  Bushwick (Bushwick Collective murals, L train), Coney Island (Cyclone, boardwalk, aquarium).
- Queens: Astoria (Greek heritage, food, Museum of the Moving Image), Long Island City
  (Gantry Plaza, MoMA PS1, Noguchi), Flushing (best Chinese food, Unisphere, US Open),
  Jackson Heights (Little India, 74th St, among the most linguistically diverse places on earth).
- Bronx: Yankee Stadium/Concourse (Grand Concourse Art Deco, free Bronx Museum),
  Bronx Zoo/Belmont (265-acre zoo, NY Botanical Garden), Arthur Avenue (Little Italy,
  retail market, Mike's Deli, Zero Otto Nove).
- Staten Island: St. George (ferry, museum, Empire Outlets), Snug Harbor (83-acre arts
  campus, Chinese Scholar's Garden), free ferry with the best Statue of Liberty view.
