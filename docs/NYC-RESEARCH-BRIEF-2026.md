- Annual: ~50 in / 1,272 mm precipitation, ~125 wet days, ~2,535 sunshine hours (~58% of possible; July sunniest, Dec dullest).
- Source (table): [timeanddate.com Central Park climate, 1992–2021](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/usa/new-york/climate); cross-check against [NWS New York (OKX) climate normals](https://www.weather.gov/wrh/Climate?wfo=okx). NWS 1991–2020 normals run ~1°F cooler in Jan–Feb (Jan 39/27).
- Best months: **mid-Apr–mid-Jun** (60s–70s °F, parks at their best, before summer humidity) and **Sep–early Oct** (warm, dry-ish, fewest wet days in Sep). **Dec** for the holiday atmosphere, not the weather. Avoid **Jul–Aug** (humid, 84°F highs, storm-prone) unless beaches/AC are the plan; Jan–Feb is cheapest and coldest.

## 4. Free things (10+)

- **Staten Island Ferry** — free, 24/7, every ~30 min, 25 min each way; best free Statue of Liberty/harbour view; board at Whitehall.
- **High Line** — free elevated park, Gansevoort→34th St; go before 10am.
- **Brooklyn Bridge + Brooklyn Bridge Park** — free; walk Brooklyn→Manhattan before 9am.
- **Central Park** — free; Bethesda Terrace, the Ramble, North Woods, Conservatory Garden.
- **Grand Central Terminal** — free; Main Concourse, Whispering Gallery, 42nd St facade at dusk.
- **The Met** — $30 adult; **pay-what-you-wish for NY State residents** (and NY/NJ/CT students) — no free day; open Sun–Tue & Thu 10–5, Fri–Sat 10–9, **closed Wed**. [engage.metmuseum.org/admission](https://engage.metmuseum.org/admission)
- **AMNH** — $28 general; **pay-what-you-wish for NY/NJ/CT residents**; daily 10–5:30. [amnh.org](https://www.amnh.org/plan-your-visit)
- **MoMA** — $30; **UNIQLO Free Friday Nights 5:30–8:30pm, NY State residents only** (timed ticket in advance, bring ID). [moma.org/visit](https://www.moma.org/visit/)
- **Whitney** — **Free Friday Nights 5–10pm for everyone**, free Second Sundays, free every day for ages 25 and under. [whitney.org free admission](https://owl.whitney.org/visit/free-admission)
- **Brooklyn Museum** — suggested admission/pay-what-you-wish; free **First Saturday** evenings (5–10pm) (verify current). [brooklynmuseum.org](https://www.brooklynmuseum.org/visit/hours_admission)
- **Bronx Museum** — **always free**, Wed–Sun 11–6, 1040 Grand Concourse. [bronxmuseum.org/visit](https://bronxmuseum.org/visit/)
- **9/11 Memorial plaza** — free daily 8am–8pm; museum $24–36, and **free for New Yorkers Sundays 4–7pm** (ID required) — this replaced the old Monday-evening window. [911memorial.org/visit/NYFS](https://911memorial.org/visit/NYFS)

## 5. Neighbourhoods

**Manhattan**

- **Midtown** — Office canyons, Times Square, Broadway and Rockefeller Center; the default base for first-timers and theatre-goers, though crowded and short on local character. Do: Top of the Rock at dusk, Grand Central, Bryant Park.
- **Lower Manhattan** — Financial District, 9/11 Memorial, Wall Street, Battery Park and the ferry terminals; suits history and harbour days. Do: 9/11 Memorial, Stone Street for a drink, Staten Island Ferry.
- **SoHo** — Cast-iron facades on cobblestone streets, now a mix of global chains, boutiques and galleries; suits shoppers and architecture buffs. Do: walk Greene/Prince/Broome, slice at Prince Street Pizza.
- **Greenwich Village** — Brownstones, NYU, jazz basements and Washington Square; suits wanderers and comedy/nightlife. Do: Washington Square Arch, Comedy Cellar, Bleecker Street.
- **Upper East Side** — Museum Mile, pre-war apartments, Madison Avenue; suits museum-goers and families. Do: the Met, Guggenheim, Conservatory Garden.
- **Upper West Side** — Brownstones, Lincoln Center, Zabar's and the park's west side; suits families and culture hounds. Do: AMNH, Lincoln Center, Riverside Park.
- **Harlem** — Brownstones, the Apollo, 125th Street and deep soul-food/gospel history; suits music and food travellers. Do: Apollo (amateur night), Sylvia's, Strivers' Row.

**Brooklyn**

- **Williamsburg** — Waterfront towers, Bedford Avenue bars, music venues and Domino Park; suits nightlife and skyline views. Do: Smorgasburg (Sat, Marsha P. Johnson Park), Domino Park, Bedford Ave.
- **DUMBO** — Cobblestones, converted warehouses, tech offices and the Washington Street bridge view; suits photographers and couples. Do: Brooklyn Bridge Park, Jane's Carousel, Washington St.
- **Park Slope** — Family brownstones, Prospect Park and restaurant-lined 5th/7th Avenues; suits a slower, local weekend. Do: Prospect Park, Grand Army Plaza greenmarket, Brooklyn Museum.
- **Bushwick** — Warehouses, the Bushwick Collective murals and DIY nightlife; suits street-art and club people. Do: mural walk off Jefferson St, Roberta's.
- **Coney Island** — Boardwalk, beach, Cyclone and Wonder Wheel; a summer half-day, bleak off-season. Do: Cyclone (~$12), boardwalk, NY Aquarium.

**Queens**

- **Astoria** — Greek heritage, bakeries, Ditmars cafés and Astoria Park; suits food-focused locals. Do: Taverna Kyclades, Museum of the Moving Image, Noguchi Museum.
- **Long Island City** — Glass towers, Gantry Plaza and MoMA PS1, with the best close-up skyline view. Do: Gantry Plaza at sunset, PS1.
- **Flushing** — The city's biggest Chinatown, Flushing Meadows Park and the Unisphere; suits serious eaters. Do: New World Mall food court, dumplings, Queens Museum.
- **Jackson Heights** — South Asian and Latin American streets around 74th St, among the most linguistically diverse places on earth. Do: Little India lunch, Patel Brothers, Diversity Plaza.

**Bronx**

- **Yankee Stadium / Concourse** — Game days plus Grand Concourse Art Deco and the free Bronx Museum. Do: Yankees game, Bronx Museum, 161st St.
- **Bronx Zoo / Belmont** — 265-acre zoo, NY Botanical Garden, and Belmont's Little Italy next door. Do: zoo, NYBG, Arthur Avenue.
- **Arthur Avenue** — The real Little Italy: markets, bakeries, salumerias. Do: Arthur Avenue Retail Market, Mike's Deli, Zero Otto Nove.

**Staten Island**

- **St. George** — Ferry terminal, Empire Outlets, ballpark and the walkable waterfront; the ferry ride is the attraction. Do: free ferry, Staten Island Museum.
- **Snug Harbor** — 83-acre cultural campus with botanical garden, Noble Maritime and a Chinese Scholar's Garden. Do: Scholar's Garden (~$5), grounds walk.

## 6. Budget (per person per day, 2026)

- **Budget $130–200** — hostel dorm/private or outer-borough budget hotel $90–220; food $40–60 (bagels, slices, halal); transit $12 (OMNY capped, 12 taps then free); sights $15–25.
- **Mid-range $350–550** — hotel $300–450; food $90–140; transit $15; sights $50–80 (one observation deck + museum).
- **Upscale $800–1,300+** — hotel $600–1,000+; food $200–350; transit $40–60 (taxis/rideshare); sights $100–200 (private tours, Broadway premium).
- Hotel nightly by category: hostel dorm **$60–120**; budget **$180–280**; mid **$300–500**; upscale **$600–1,200+**. Add **~14.75% tax + $3.50/night** occupancy fee (not in quoted rates).
- Broadway: average paid ticket **~$131**; TKTS/broadway rush cuts that sharply (see §7).
- Transit: subway/bus **$3.00**, express bus $7.25; 12 fares in 7 days then free with the same OMNY card/device (≈$36/week). [mta.info/fares](https://www.mta.info/fares-tolls)

## 7. Practical

- **Tipping** — 18–22% sit-down (20% standard), $2–3/drink at bars, $1–2 coat check, 15–20% taxis, $3–5/night housekeeping; counter service optional.
- **Accessibility** — only **~150 of 472** subway stations are step-free; all buses are wheelchair-accessible. Check the [MTA accessible stations list](https://www.mta.info/accessibility/stations) and [live elevator status](https://www.mta.info/elevator-escalator-status) before travelling; use the AutoGate at accessible stations. Key step-free stations: Times Sq–42 St, Grand Central, Fulton St, Atlantic Av–Barclays, Flushing–Main St, 161 St–Yankee Stadium, Coney Island–Stillwell Av.
- **Free WiFi** — LinkNYC kiosks (24/7, citywide), subway platform WiFi, NYPL/BPL/Queens libraries, Bryant Park, Moynihan and Penn.
- **eSIM** — buy a US data eSIM before landing (Airalo, Holafly, Nomad, or your carrier's roaming add-on); keeps your home number active for 2FA via dual SIM.
- **Apps** — MTA (service + OMNY), Citymapper/Transit, Google/Apple Maps, TodayTix + TKTS, Resy/OpenTable, Curb (yellow cabs), Uber/Lyft, Bloomberg Connects (museum guides).
- **Luggage storage** — Stasher/Bounce/Radical Storage ~$6–12 per bag per day (book ahead); hotel bell desks usually free on check-in/out days; no lockers at the major museums.
- **Broadway cheap tickets** — **TKTS** same-day + next-day matinees up to 50% off; Times Sq booth Mon/Tue/Fri 3–8pm, Wed/Thu/Sat/Sun 11am–8pm; Lincoln Center booth Tue–Sat 11am–6pm; fees apply. [tdf.org/tkts](https://www.tdf.org/discount-ticket-programs/tkts-by-tdf/tkts-live/) **Digital lotteries**: Broadway Direct, Lucky Seat, TodayTix (enter 1–7 days ahead). **Rush**: box office at opening or TodayTix digital rush; some shows still hold in-person lotteries. Standing room only sold at the box office on the day.
- **Safety** — statistically safe for a big city; phone out of sight on crowded trains and in Times Sq, bag in front, ride in the front car with the conductor late at night, ignore CD/"free" bracelet and costumed-character hustles, never buy Statue of Liberty tickets from touts. 911 emergency, 311 non-emergency.
