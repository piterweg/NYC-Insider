How your choice works

Your answer is stored locally in your browser under the key nyctrove-consent. It is not sent to us and it is not tied to your identity. Choosing here has the same effect as the banner you see on your first visit.

If you clear this site's data, block local storage, or open the site in a private window, the banner will appear again and analytics stays off until you answer.

Changing your mind elsewhere

You can also control cookies from your browser settings — most browsers let you block third-party cookies or clear them for a single site. Google provides a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics across all sites; it works independently of this page.

Because the site is served from the EU and the UK, our legal basis for analytics is your consent. Declining has no effect on what you can read or book.
