Brooklyn · Williamsburg

Williamsburg, where Brooklyn looks at Manhattan

Williamsburg is Brooklyn's most talked-about neighbourhood and its most misunderstood. Behind the brunch queues there is a waterfront with the best skyline view in the city, a Saturday food market and streets that are pleasant on a weekday morning.

First impressions

Waterfront views and a very good Saturday

The neighbourhood runs along the East River north of the Williamsburg Bridge, and the waterfront is the reason to come: a park with lawns, a ferry stop and a skyline view that costs nothing.

Start at Bedford Avenue and walk west to the river, then north along the waterfront to Domino Park. The walk takes half an hour; the view takes longer. On a Saturday in summer, Smorgasburg at Marsha P. Johnson State Park turns the waterfront into the city's best open-air lunch.

The bars and restaurants run along Bedford and the side streets toward the bridge. It is a young, loud neighbourhood at night and a quiet one before noon; both versions are worth seeing, but not on the same visit if you want to eat well without queuing.

A Saturday in Williamsburg

Bedford first, then the river, then the bridge or a long lunch.

  • 10:00 — Bedford Avenue

    Coffee and a walk along the main street before the brunch queues form.

  • 11:30 — The waterfront

    Walk west and north to Domino Park for the skyline and the cranes.

  • 12:30 — Smorgasburg

    On a Saturday in summer, eat your way through the stalls at the waterfront park.

  • 15:00 — The Williamsburg Bridge

    Walk off lunch with the crossing into Manhattan, or stay and explore the side streets.

  • 18:00 — Sunset and dinner

    The waterfront is best at sunset; afterwards, the restaurants along Bedford and the side streets.

Practical

Getting there and getting it right

The L to Bedford Avenue is the classic route, about 10 minutes from Union Square. The J/M/Z stop at Marcy Avenue, a 10-minute walk south of Bedford. The NYC Ferry ($5 one-way) connects the Williamsburg waterfront to Manhattan and is the nicest way over on a fine day. Note that the L has frequent weekend work; check the MTA before you rely on it.

Weekday mornings are calm and best for walking. Saturday is the day to come for the market and the waterfront, though it is also the busiest. Evenings are for bars and restaurants; if you want a quiet dinner, book or eat early.

◆

Walk the waterfront, not just Bedford

Bedford is the shopping street. The river path and Domino Park are the reason to stay longer than brunch.

◷

Weekday for a calm visit

If you want the neighbourhood without the queues, come Tuesday to Thursday; the difference is considerable.

☂

Check the L train

Weekend closures and diversions are common on the L. Have a plan B via the J/M/Z or the ferry.

Questions

Williamsburg questions

Is Williamsburg worth visiting on a short trip?

Yes, if you want the Brooklyn waterfront and a sense of how young New York lives. Combine it with DUMBO or a bridge walk rather than making it the only Brooklyn stop.

How do I get to Williamsburg?

The L train to Bedford Avenue is the classic route, about 10 minutes from Union Square. The J/M/Z stop at Marcy Avenue, and the NYC Ferry connects the Williamsburg waterfront to Manhattan.

When is Williamsburg busiest?

Saturday afternoons and evenings are the busiest, especially when the waterfront market is running. Weekday mornings are much calmer and better for walking.

Where is the best skyline view in Williamsburg?

The waterfront parks, from Domino Park south to the ferry stop, give you the Manhattan skyline directly across the river. It is best at sunset and free.
