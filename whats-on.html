What's on

What's on in New York

New York runs on a seasonal calendar: blossom in spring, free music all summer, foliage and Halloween in autumn, then a parade, a tree and a ball drop at midnight. This is the hub for the nine seasonal guides, with the dates worth planning around.

How the year works

The city has a rhythm, and it is bookable

Most New York trips are planned around the weather. The better ones are planned around the calendar: the weeks when the city does something specific, and the weeks when it is simply itself.

The year divides into useful blocks. March to May is blossom and Easter crowds; late May to early September moves the city outdoors for free concerts, beaches and the US Open; September to early November is the best walking weather of the year, with foliage following in late October; and late November to early January is the holiday season, with the highest hotel rates of the year.

Each guide covers one season in detail: what happens, when to arrive, what to book and what the crowds are like. Where a date is not yet announced, we say so and point you to the official source.

Booking windows

What to book, and how far ahead

New York rewards early booking most in the holiday weeks and least in January. This is the rough order in which things sell out.

Booking windows for New York seasonal events and holidays
BookingHow far ahead
Thanksgiving and Christmas hotelsFour to six months
New Year's Eve tables and hotel packagesFour to six months
Restaurant Week reservationsAs soon as each edition opens
US Open ticketsWhen the official sale opens, months ahead
Broadway over the holidaysTwo to three months
Free summer concertsNo booking for most; some ticketed shows

These are rules of thumb, not guarantees. The last week of December behaves differently every year.

◆

Holiday weeks cost more

Hotel rates in the weeks around Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year are the highest of the year. If your dates are flexible, the first two weeks of December give you the atmosphere for less.

☂

Free events still need planning

The parade and the ball drop are free but demand hours of standing and an early arrival. Decide whether that trade is worth it before building a day around it.

◷

January is the value month

After New Year, the city empties and prices fall. Restaurant Week starts in late January and museums are quiet.

Questions

Seasonal calendar questions

What is the biggest event in New York in November?

The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade on 26 November 2026, running from Central Park West and 77th Street to Herald Square between 8:30am and noon. It is free.

When does the Christmas season start in New York?

Bryant Park Winter Village opens on 28 October 2026 and the Rockefeller Center tree is lit on 2 December 2026, staying up until about 9 January 2027. Early December is the sweet spot: less crowded than the final week of the year.

Do I need tickets for New Year's Eve in Times Square?

No. The ball drop is free and unticketed. Arrive in the early afternoon, expect security screening and plan to stand for hours in the cold.

When is NYC Restaurant Week 2027?

Restaurant Week runs in late January to mid-February and mid-July to mid-August, with prix-fixe menus at $30, $45 and $60. The exact 2027 dates are still to be announced — check the official NYC Tourism page.

What happens in New York in summer?

Free concerts at SummerStage, BRIC Celebrate Brooklyn! and Lincoln Center Summer for the City, long evenings in the parks, beaches and the US Open at the end of August. July and August are hot and humid, so plan outdoor time for mornings and evenings.

When is the US Open in 2027?

The 2026 edition ran from 30 August to 13 September. The 2027 dates are TBA, so check the official US Open site before booking travel around it.
