New York in May: the best month for walking

May is one of the two best months to visit: warm without being hot, green in every park, and light until well into the evening. It is busy, but the city is built for it — outdoor tables, long walks and free things to do.

The short version

Warm days, long evenings, no heat

May gets the weather right: highs in the low 70s, cool evenings and none of the humidity that arrives in June. The city's outdoor life is fully open, from the parks to the waterfront.

Average highs reach 72°F (22°C) and lows sit around 54°F (12°C), with about fourteen and a half hours of daylight. Rain is still possible, but the wet days are warm and brief compared with spring.

The month is busy with visitors and locals alike, and the parks and waterfronts fill at weekends. Book the big sights ahead, but leave room for the free things: the Staten Island Ferry, the High Line and a long afternoon in Central Park.

What's on

What is on in May

Fleet Week, long evenings and the outdoor season in full swing.

  • Usually late May — Fleet Week

    Warships and sailors fill the waterfront. The 2026 edition was replaced by the International Naval Review in July; check the official site for current dates.

  • All month — The best-weather window

    Mid-April to mid-June is one of the year's two best stretches; May is the warmest part of it.

  • All month — Long evenings

    With about fourteen and a half hours of daylight, the city stays usable until well after dinner.

How to spend it

How to spend a May day

Use the long daylight: park in the morning, waterfront at midday, outdoor dinner in the evening.

Morning

Central Park before the crowds

Enter at 72nd or 86th Street; the park is calm before 10am.

Midday

The High Line and Chelsea

Walk south to north and finish at Hudson Yards for lunch.

Evening

The waterfront

Brooklyn Bridge Park or the Staten Island Ferry terminal at sunset; the long evenings are the point.

Practical

May planning notes

May is busy: the weather brings everyone out, and the big sights sell out at weekends. Book observation decks and museums ahead, and use weekday mornings for the park.

Light layers, comfortable walking shoes and sunglasses, plus a light jacket for the evening. May days can feel like summer, but evenings stay cool.

Questions

Month questions

Is May a good time to visit New York?

Yes, one of the best. Warm days, cool evenings and long daylight, with humidity still manageable before summer.

How warm is New York in May?

Average highs are around 72°F (22°C) and lows around 54°F (12°C). Evenings are cool enough for a jacket.

What is on in May?

Fleet Week is usually late May, though the 2026 edition was replaced by the International Naval Review in July. Check the official site for current dates.

Is May busy in New York?

Yes. The good weather brings crowds, especially at weekends. Book the big sights ahead and use weekday mornings for the park.
