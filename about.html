What this is

A guide built the slow way

A travel guide to New York City — all five boroughs, the whole trip, not just the postcard sights.

You will find day-by-day plans for three, five and seven days, borough guides that treat all five boroughs as destinations, honest comparisons of passes and tickets, and the practical detail — transit, budgets, timing — that decides whether a good plan survives contact with the city.

We are not a booking site: if a page recommends something, we think it is worth your time or money.

◇

No sponsored rankings

No venue, hotel or tour operator can pay to appear in a guide, itinerary or best-of list. Labelled partnerships are stated on the page.

◷

Dated, not timeless

Every guide shows when it was last checked. New York changes fast, and a guide that pretends otherwise is worse than useless.

✎

Corrections welcome

Found something stale or wrong? Tell us and we will check it against the official source.

Editorial policy

How we work

Four rules, applied to every page on the site.

  • 01

    Independence comes first

    The editorial side is not for sale. We do not accept payment for inclusion, ranking or a favourable review.

  • 02

    We describe places we can vouch for

    We do not invent restaurants, hotels or tours. If we cannot check a place, we describe the category and area instead.

  • 03

    We show our working

    Prices are written as "from" or "around", with the date checked. Hours and fares are checked against the operator's own page.

  • 04

    We correct in public

    When we get something wrong, we fix it. Corrections are the most useful email a reader can send us.

Money

How we make money — and what it does not buy

NYCTrove is free to read and has no paywall. It is funded by affiliate commissions and clearly labelled partnerships.

Some links to ticket sellers, pass operators and booking sites are affiliate links. If you buy through one, the partner pays us a small commission at no extra cost to you. The price is the same, and the recommendation is not for sale.

We do not run sponsored best-of lists or accept payment for a place to appear in an itinerary. Any paid collaboration is labelled before you read it.

The full disclosure sits in our terms of use; data handling is in the privacy policy.

◆

The short version

If we recommend a ticket or tour, we may earn from the link — and it should still be the right call for your trip. That is the deal we hold ourselves to.

How we keep prices current

Prices are the fastest-moving thing in any guide. Here is what we re-check.

What NYCTrove re-checks and how often
WhatHow often we checkWhat the page tells you
Attraction, pass and tour pricesBefore each season and when an operator changes pricingA from price, the date checked and a note that dynamic pricing can push the real cost higher.
Opening hours and closuresMonthly, and when a reader flags a changeUsual hours and known closures. Confirm on the venue's own site before a special trip.
Transit fares and rulesWhen the MTA or an operator announces a changeThe current fare, the date it took effect, and cheaper alternatives.
Events and seasonal datesAt the start of each seasonDates we have verified, or a clear note that next year's are not announced yet.
