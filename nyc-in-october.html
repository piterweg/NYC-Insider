New York in October: foliage and crisp air

October is autumn at its best: highs in the mid-60s, low humidity and the park turning colour towards the end of the month. It is a good month for walking, with summer crowds gone and the holiday season not yet arrived.

The short version

The month the park turns

October is the most comfortable month for walking: cool enough for a coat, warm enough for a long afternoon, and dry more often than not. The foliage is the headline, but the whole city is at an easy pace.

Average highs reach 64°F (18°C) with lows around 52°F (11°C), and daylight drops to about eleven hours. The first half of the month often feels like a mild September; the second half brings the colour and the cold evenings.

Foliage peaks between late October and mid-November, and Bryant Park's Winter Village opens on 28 October 2026 — the skating rink's admission is free, with rentals paid. It is a good month to combine long walks with the museums as the weather turns.

What's on

What is on in October

Foliage season and the first holiday market of the year.

  • Late October to mid-November — Fall foliage

    The park turns colour from late October; the peak is usually early to mid-November. Weekday mornings are calmest.

  • From 28 October 2026 — Bryant Park Winter Village

    The skating rink and market open for the season; rink admission is free, skate rental is paid.

  • All month — Long walking weather

    October is one of the best months for the bridge walk, the High Line and the neighbourhoods; book outdoor tables while you can.

How to spend it

How to spend an October day

Walk in the morning, museum at midday and an early dinner as the evenings turn cold.

Morning

A park walk

Start at 72nd Street and walk north; the light and the colour are best before the afternoon crowds.

Midday

A museum

As the weather cools, the big museums become the natural midday stop.

Evening

An early dinner

The evenings are cold but the restaurants are less busy than in summer; book a neighbourhood table.

Practical

October planning notes

Crowds are moderate: summer is over and the holiday season has not started, except for the Winter Village opening and the first foliage weekends. Weekdays are calm everywhere.

A warm jacket or coat, layers and closed shoes for wet leaves. An umbrella is worth carrying; October rain is less frequent but the streets get slippery.

◷

Time the foliage

Peak colour usually arrives in early to mid-November. Late October is early but quieter; check the park's foliage updates.

◆

Use the last warm days

The first half of October is often mild enough for outdoor dining and long walks; make the most of it.

☂

Book the Winter Village early

The rink and market get busy as the season opens; go on a weekday.

Questions

Month questions

Is October a good time to visit New York?

Yes. Cool, dry walking weather and the start of the foliage season, with fewer crowds than summer.

When is fall foliage in New York?

Usually from late October, peaking in early to mid-November. The exact timing shifts with the weather, so check the park's updates before planning around it.

How cold is New York in October?

Average highs are around 64°F (18°C) and lows around 52°F (11°C), with colder evenings later in the month.

What is on in October?

Foliage season and the opening of Bryant Park's Winter Village on 28 October 2026.
